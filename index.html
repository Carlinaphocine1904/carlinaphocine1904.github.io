VFA FOOTBALL • SERIES 2

The Reckoning

Records are low. The pressure is high. Everyone has something to prove.

It's Series 2, tensions are high and records are low. Everybody has something to prove. With the Victorious Bowl waiting at the end, the question is simple: who is ready to take control?

THE FRONT-RUNNER

Houston — Togoz

Will Houston's Togoz take the charge? He is putting up historic numbers, sitting at 16 touchdowns to just a single interception. The production is impossible to ignore, and Togoz is looking to turn that historic start into a run all the way to the Victorious Bowl.

THE CHALLENGE

Charlotte — Rico, Xim & Tfrx

But the people standing in Houston's path are dangerous. Charlotte's Rico is a proven LFG quarterback who has already made multiple Legendary Bowls. Add Xim, Tfrx, and the rest of Charlotte's elite players, and we are going to be in for a season, folks.

THE UNDERDOG

Minneapolis — Hex

But one story isn't mentioned nearly enough: Minneapolis and Hex. They were 15.5-point underdogs playing Antman, and somehow found a way to come out with the win. They completely shattered expectations, even taking down Charlotte.

Now Minneapolis has everyone's attention. The underdog story is no longer just a story — it is a warning to everyone else in the league.

THE SEASON AWAITS

How this Series pans out will define the whole season. Houston has the numbers, Charlotte has the experience, and Minneapolis has already shown that expectations can be shattered. Good luck.
